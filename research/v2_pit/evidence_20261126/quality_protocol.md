# 2021Q1 field QC, frozen before opening returns
The historical denominator is all 782 ordinary shares in the March 31 KRX snapshot. A firm with no filing, a different year-end, a later exit, suspended trading, or standalone-only reporting stays in the census denominator. An unmatched issuer, ambiguous scope, unknown unit or missing EPS may never be assigned zero. Report matching, filing capture, valid field pairs and eligibility separately. April–June is a filing search window, not proof of completeness for late reports or corrections.

Use receipts and conservative next-session availability. Same-document prior-year comparators do not change the recorded value of an earlier release. Match receipt, original/correction, DART issuer, KRX code and ISIN, quarter dates, consolidated group including discontinued operations, KRW units and common basic EPS. A note can resolve a mislabelled XBRL unit or taxonomy extension only when its scope and share class are explicit. Do not silently convert financial-industry gross income or net interest income into nonfinancial sales. For non-December fiscal year-ends use dates and the filing type appropriate to the issuer; unresolved cases remain in missingness.

Before viewing outcomes, sample six issuers by ascending SHA256 of the UTF-8 string 'mr-v2-qc1:' + historical ISIN. Add KB Financial (105560) and Mirae Asset Securities (006800) as two financial accounting challenges. Do not replace missing draws. Verify current and prior values against the displayed original income statement/notes including units, scope and share class. Keep the earlier convenience pilot separate.

Expansion to 2017–21 requires >=90% unambiguous receipt/identity coverage in the full March census and >=80% valid same-basis field-pair coverage for each signal in its declared applicable historical group. Publish the all-782 denominators too. In the eight-issuer review require zero accepted material extraction/timing errors; missing or intentionally quarantined fields reduce coverage. Also require source/version checks on historical exits, an amendment, an EPS/share-basis change, industry-specific concepts, and a reproducible way of discovering earlier public values. These are operational thresholds chosen for this study, not estimated accuracy or a guarantee against selection bias. Without full version discovery an explicitly report-only study could use the receipt date conservatively but must not label it the first market disclosure. No outcomes were used to choose these gates.
