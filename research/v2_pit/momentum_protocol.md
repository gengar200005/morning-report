# Stage 5 frozen signal protocol

- Formation is the first session of each calendar month in 2017–2023. Entry is the following official open. For horizon H, the exit mark is the Hth session close counting entry as session one. A window crossing either phase's end is recorded as censored before outcome evaluation. No 2024+ date is queried for scores or outcomes.
- Common universe uses same-day KOSPI ordinary membership and 253 uninterrupted verified index observations including formation. All signals share that universe. M1 = P[t−21]/P[t−252]−1, M2 = P[t−126]/P[t−252]−1, M3 = P[t−21]/P[t−126]−1. M4 = P[t]/max(P[t−252:t])−1 against the preceding 252 closes, so positive values identify breakouts. M5 = mean of same-date percentile ranks of price distance to MA200 and MA200 growth over 22 sessions. Reuse the Stage 4 exact trailing averages ending at t−1 and t−23. Also test the literal positive-distance/positive-slope subset vs M1 top quintile separately.
- High score first, tie break ISIN. Top decile is nested inside top quintile. Middle is the residual 60%. Compare to the remainder, all eligible names, and non-top names in same-date cap/turnover tercile cells. Controls are equal weighted per occupied cell, and top names weight cells. Matching ignores outcomes. Missing cells remain visible. Liquidity sensitivity uses the upper two turnover terciles. Market regimes use the sign of the trailing 60-session KODEX 200 close return observable at formation.
- Primary outcome is split-adjusted price, gross of all costs. Require positive valid entry and exit bars. Check all forward links against the previous observed close and KRX implied reference. Verified integer splits adjust share count and reference, including known halt/reopening bridges. Other unresolved actions, missing exit/exit from PIT, and unverified links remain explicit. A halt followed by a verified unchanged-unit reference may be bridged for endpoint valuation, never for entry or exit. Gross economic sensitivity credits documented cash rights on ex-date (excluding entry morning) without reinvestment. Dividend inventory is incomplete, so this is partial economic return. ETF comparison uses raw opening and closing prices and, separately, archived issuer cash rights. Unresolved post-entry cases get ±20 percentage points relative to the observed same-date universe mean as missing-outcome stress (floored at −100%); this is a scenario, not an estimate or legal recovery assumption. Unavailable entry never receives an imputed return.
- Equal weight dates, not pooled security observations. Primary inferential family is five signals × four horizons, top quintile versus remainder. Centered circular six-month block bootstrap on monthly values (5,000 draws, fixed seed), two-sided p, 95% percentile interval, Holm within each phase. Secondary paired hypotheses M2−M3 on monthly spreads and literal MA gate−M1 on monthly group returns, exploratory. Report regime/year dependence, delete-one-year, remove five largest absolute name contributions, economic and missingness sensitivity. A candidate needs positive 60-session spread in both phases, positive matched spread in both, validation Holm p<.05 at 60 sessions, and no sign flip under missingness stress or concentration diagnostics. This reused historical selection period is temporally separate and is not an untouched holdout.
