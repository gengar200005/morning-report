# Stage 6 frozen M4 position sizing comparison

Each account begins at KRW 10 million. Independent development and validation accounts start at the first Stage 5 formation with an eligible universe (2017-02-01, 2022-01-03); a continuous account uses the development start and validation end. Mark through 2021-12-30 or 2023-12-28. Use calendar-only T+2 sessions outside those boundaries as needed. No post-2023 security or ETF outcomes.

Select M4 top 1/5/10 using frozen Stage 5 eligible formations and ISIN tie order. At the monthly first close, divide known ledger equity by K. Unresolved assets with a previously observed close retain that close for sizing only; unpriced entitlements add no sizing budget and remain explicitly unresolved. Use current closing price to compute integer target shares, fixed for the monthly rebalance. Submit sells first, then buys in rank order for the delta; cap a buy at its target budget after marked existing shares and at settled buying power net of reservations. Pending stock rights count toward the target and cannot be sold before delivery. Use E2 +2% limit on each prior close; orders fill at next open only, with independent 1% and 0.1% all-or-none volume scenarios. The daily volume test is retrospective, never a sizing input. Retry outstanding deltas through five sessions after formation, including after T+2; no orders between windows. Zero credit interest in all accounts. Existing corporate-action ledger and exploratory valuation are retained. Use existing documented Stage 4.1 splits and bonus schedules equally across accounts. Unresolved NAV cannot certify full-path CAGR/MDD; report raw ledger decomposition, independently reconciled observed prefix with synchronized ETF and no fictitious liquidation.

Controls randomly select distinct non-target stocks within the same date cap/turnover tercile cell per corresponding M4 rank slot; expand to minimum Manhattan distance across cells if necessary. Five seeds fixed at 1101–1105, selected without outcomes. Use identical dates, costs, retry and liquidity rules. Report coverage and distribution, keeping unresolved runs out of numerical return quantiles with counts.

ETF uses same initial cash, start, execution attempts and volume caps, raw opens/closes, five-won order ticks and documented issuer gross distribution entitlements; one initial buy, no reinvestment. Include all confirmed payouts and deduct modeled buy costs; treat close of first session as the starting account mark. Report separately realized gross price contributions by name and descriptive attribution, not optimized exclusion. Candidate requires complete research NAV and positive ETF CAGR spread in both independent windows at both caps; otherwise no finalist. Save orders, fills, corporate action marks, hashes, and annual returns. No 2024+ prices, rankings, signals or outcomes.
