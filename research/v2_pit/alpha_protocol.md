# Stage 7 frozen alpha attribution

Inputs: hash-verified Stage 5 formation/outcome cache and Stage 6 monthly Top10/ETF tapes. Use 2017–2021 development and 2022–2023 reused exploratory period. Continuous replay is descriptive. Never evaluate 2024+ observations.

Breadth: sort M4 descending then ISIN; cumulative ceil(top 1/5/10/20%) and ten disjoint deciles. Outcome comparisons use identical date/horizon, nonselected universe, ETF, and cap/turnover tercile matching (report coverage). Compute 5/20/60/120-session outcomes, daily-volume proxies from formation turnover and available cap, trailing 20-session volatility, unresolved outcome causes, upper-five absolute name-contribution deletion, and delete-one-year. Within M4 top20, split trailing 20-session close return by equal-count thirds, compare bottom and top third on 20/60/120 outcomes, directly and with matched cells. Boundary ties by ISIN. No threshold search.

Regimes at formation close: ETF trend 60-session return positive; volatility annualized prior 60-session daily log-return SD above 20%; breadth at least 20% of the ranked universe within 5% of 52-week high; rebound when ETF prior 60-session return is negative and trailing 20-session return is positive. Use one binary split at a time, report both states and state-minus-state contrasts; no joint rule search. For missing ETF lookback keep unknown. Report all, matched, and ETF spreads separately. Inference: equal formation-month weight, 5,000 circular 6-month block resamples retaining empty state months, 95% percentile intervals, centered two-sided p, Holm across all main spreads, paired reversals and regime contrasts per phase. Low-coverage cells remain descriptive. Economic and missing outcome stress are separate.

Accounts: fixed Top10, same PIT ranking, base cash, costs and E2 caps, five fixed matched-control seeds and ETF. Use cached Stage 6 monthly rebalance. Compare one block with 100% budget, restarting at first monthly formation after the prior block's 120-session span and liquidation, and monthly staggered entry with one sixth of marked equity per formation. Each buy lot schedules first sale at the next available open after 120 sessions including its fill session. Attempt exit every available open until filled; no replacement inside a cohort. Each formation gets five entry attempts, only settled funds, and expiry-origin lots sold FIFO among expired units. Net orders per name; exits take priority. Lot bookkeeping allocates documented corporate share changes to originating lots, without valuing unresolved rights. Trailing incomplete cohorts are marked on the common end date. Report actual fill-to-sale duration of closed shares, turnover proxy, execution and known NAV with matched dates. Fixed 120 was chosen after Stage 5. It is exploratory.

Candidate requires reproducible positive matched predictive spread and ETF excess in both phases, favorable development Holm p<.05, and cost-adjusted positive ETF CAGR spread in both independent periods at both caps with no unexplained NAV. No re-tuning or final holdout access.
