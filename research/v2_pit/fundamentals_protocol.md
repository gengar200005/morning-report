# Stage 8 small earnings and flow feasibility pilot

Audit targets are Samsung Electronics 005930, LG Electronics 066570, SK hynix 000660 (2021Q1 and 2023Q1), and historical exits Hanjin Shipping 117930 (2016Q3, identity/availability only) and Korea Glass 002000 (2018Q3). This convenience sample is stratified for document and exit checks, not representative for alpha. Record all planned rows, including rejected ones. Original receipt and correction endpoints are distinct from corporate IR date evidence. A public issuer release can support a dated event pilot, but cannot certify complete DART revision coverage. If an earlier preliminary release carried numbers, preserve and label versions separately; this pilot uses the dated release with YoY comparison and documents that earlier market awareness can exist.

Use consolidated quarter-only revenue and operating profit, same currency/scope and prior-year quarter as published in the dated document. Improvement means positive YoY operating margin change (percentage points). Retain YoY revenue and operating income growth only if the previous base is positive. Freeze M4 strong as the top 20% of that day's existing common PIT eligible ranks. Cells A/B/C/D mean price-only, earnings-only, both strong, neither strong. Keep unavailable cells empty. Measure next KRX session open to 20/60/120-session close, with date-only publication timing always waiting until the next session, bounded by phase. Same-day close may be used for M4 since entry is on a later day. Reuse Stage 5 economic outcome validity rules. Compare with ETF raw and partial economic returns, and same-day cap/turnover tercile matched stocks with known price data; matching happens before outcomes. Publish missingness, timestamp precision, sources and caveats. No statistical inference, subgroup optimization or promotion from six convenience events. Future sufficiently sized samples should use monthly block resampling and issuer clustering, year/name exclusions, confirmatory C−A and C−B contrasts, and a separate prospective freeze.
